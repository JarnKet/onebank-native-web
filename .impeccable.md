## Design Context

### Users
Two groups, weighted equally:

- **Business teams**: SME owners, accountants and cashiers in Laos. They work at an office desk, on a laptop or desktop, and manage shared company accounts. Their work is structured by roles: a maker submits a payment, and one or more approvers sign it off. They come to pay suppliers and salaries, settle utility bills, write e-cheques and check the statement.
- **Families and informal groups**: households, associations and savings circles that share money. They are less expert, use the app less often, and need everything to be plain.

Both groups ask the same thing of the app: move money for a group, with the right people agreeing, and never be unsure where it went.

The interface is **Lao first**, with English, Chinese and Vietnamese as alternatives. Every label is Lao-script aware: Noto Sans Lao Looped is the only face that renders it properly at small sizes.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy, clear and warm.**

- **Trustworthy**: this is BCEL, a national bank. Amounts, statuses and who approved what are never ambiguous.
- **Clear**: one primary action per screen, plain words, and numbers set in tabular figures.
- **Warm**: rounded 20px surfaces, a friendly Lao tone and generous spacing. It reads as a helpful teller, not a terminal.

The emotional goal is calm confidence. Users should finish a transfer knowing exactly what happened and what happens next.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: a light, soft-surface product UI taken from the Figma file "OneBank Web – by Pink".
  - White cards with a 20px radius on a `#f6f6f6` ground, and one soft shadow.
  - Red (`#c11111`) is reserved for the active or primary element.
  - Navy (`#133D6B`) is the secondary accent: outlined secondary buttons, filters, active tabs.
  - No black surfaces, even where the Figma draws them.
- **Theme**: light only. It is used at a desk in daylight office hours, alongside paper invoices.
- **Brand colours** (fixed; do not change them):
  - `#c11111` for UI red fills.
  - `#DD2319` for the logo red.
  - `#133D6B` for navy.
- **Anti-references**:
  - Crypto or neon fintech: dark mode with glowing accents.
  - Legacy core-banking UI: grey, dense and tiny.
  - A generic SaaS dashboard: interchangeable stat tiles and purple accents.
  - Playful or cartoonish styling: mascots, bouncy motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Money is never ambiguous.**
   - Debits are red with a sign; credits are green with a sign.
   - The status is always spelled out in words, never shown by colour alone.
   - Figures are tabular.
   - Pending always says *who* it is waiting for.
2. **One red thing per view.** Red marks the active group tab, the current nav item and the primary action, so it stays meaningful. Secondary actions are black or grey.
3. **Show the approval chain.** Wherever a transaction appears, show who made it, who approved it, who rejected it and why. Trust in a shared account comes from that visibility.
4. **Lao first, always legible.**
   - Test every string at Lao length.
   - Don't truncate amounts.
   - Aim for 16px body text.
   - Meet WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, a visible focus ring, full keyboard operation, and respect for reduced motion.
5. **Follow the design, then fill its gaps consistently.** Where the Figma is silent (empty, error and loading states, demo affordances), extend its own vocabulary: cards, pills, the black panel. Don't invent new ones.
